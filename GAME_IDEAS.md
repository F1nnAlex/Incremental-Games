# Incremental Game Concepts

Original concepts built around the classic **points + layered resets** formula (*Antimatter Dimensions* / *Prestige Tree* / *Synergism* style), where every prestige layer introduces a distinct mechanic instead of just another flat multiplier.

Designed to fit this repository's self-contained static web stack (`index.html` + CSS + vanilla JS + `break_eternity.js`).

---

## 1. Reality Compiler *(Software & Glitching Universe Incremental)*

**The Hook:** Instead of gathering cookies or cosmic dust, you are compiling a simulated universe from raw clock cycles—fixing bugs, swapping kernel modules, and eventually forking reality itself.

* **Layer 0 — Base (`Cycles`):** Build Threads, Loops, and Cache to generate raw Cycles along your first exponential curve.
* **Layer 1 — *Refactor* (`Tokens`):** Resets Cycles and Threads to unlock a Patch Tree and **Bug Challenges** (e.g., *"Memory Leak: Cycles decay 10%/s"* or *"Strict Typing: only 1 generator tier active"*). Patching Bugs awards permanent **Hotfixes**.
* **Layer 2 — *Compile* (`Binaries`):** Introduces **Kernel Loadouts**. You have limited RAM slots and swap Modules (*Compression*, *Overclock*, *Recursion*) in and out to optimize either max Cycles or Token farming.
* **Layer 3 — *Fork* (`Branches`):** Split the simulation into parallel **Branches** running different rule modifiers simultaneously, then **Merge** them to combine peak exponents and shatter softcaps.

**What makes it original:** Crisp OS/terminal presentation where challenges are Bugs, builds are slottable RAM modules, and late-game resets play like version-controlling timelines.

## 2. Bureaucracy of Infinity *(Satirical Cosmic Paperwork Incremental)*

**The Hook:** Satirical cosmic comedy—start as an unpaid intern stamping Forms in a celestial DMV, and climb through absurd tiers of middle management until you are auditing thermodynamics and filing loopholes in the laws of physics.

* **Layer 0 — Base (`Forms`):** Stamp Forms using Interns, Rubber Stamps, and Espresso Machines.
* **Layer 1 — *File* (`Folders`):** Unlocks **Office Policies** (toggleable tradeoffs like *"Rush Order: +300% stamp speed, +50% cost scaling"*) and staff auto-hiring milestones.
* **Layer 2 — *Audit* (`Red Tape`):** Run **Compliance Audits** (restricted challenge runs like *"Zero Coffee"* or *"Tax Season"*) to stamp permanent **Approvals** onto your desk.
* **Layer 3 — *Department Split* (`Budgets`):** Unlocks three rival Departments (*Logistics*, *HR*, *R&D*). Pumping one creates a **Backlog** penalty, rewarding rotating resets across departments in deliberate cycles.
* **Layer 4 — *Executive Order* (`Loopholes`):** Rewrite the cosmic charter so penalties (*Backlog*, *Taxes*, *Cost Scaling*) flip into multipliers.

**What makes it original:** Memorable comedic flavor where red tape and penalties start as genuine friction in early layers and become your biggest power engine via Loopholes later on.

## 3. Mythogenesis *(Creation Myth & Constellation Incremental)* — **[Selected for `NewGame/`]**

**The Hook:** Shape raw Aether into the four Primordial Elements, weave Constellations across a live star map, bargain with rival Gods through Mythic Trials, and shatter the cosmos into a New Age.

* **Layer 0 — Primordial Void (`Aether`):** Spark the void manually and build a chain of Aether Conduits (*Wisp → Current → Wellspring → Nexus*).
* **Layer 1 — *Condense* (`Elemental Essence`):** Choose which **Primordial Element** (*Flame*, *Tide*, *Stone*, or *Gale*) absorbs each reset. Each element strengthens itself and feeds the *next* element clockwise (`Flame → Tide → Stone → Gale → Flame`), rewarding smart reset rotation.
* **Layer 2 — *Astral Ascent* (`Starlight`):** Spend Starlight on an **interactive Star Map** where you ignite stars and connect edges between them—unlocking adjacency synergies and geometric **Constellation Shapes** (Pairs, Triads, Elemental Bridges).
* **Layer 3 — *The Pantheon* (`Devotion`):** Unlock four Patron Deities who offer shrines, active **Blessings & Sacrifices**, and **Mythic Trials** (distinct rule-bending challenge runs).
* **Layer 4 — *Ragnarok / New Age* (`Creation Echoes`):** Collapse the old cosmos to unlock **Cosmology Laws** that bend core math, preserve lower layers, and break past the `1e308` cosmic barrier.

**What makes it original:** Combines an elemental rotation puzzle in Layer 1, a tactile connect-the-stars graph puzzle in Layer 2, and thematic Deity Trials in Layer 3.

## 4. The Hollow World *(Planetary Strata Incremental)*

**The Hook:** Chip Dust off the surface of a mysterious hollow planet, where every reset layer physically descends into a deeper stratum with brand-new mechanics.

* **Layer 0 — Base (`Dust / Ore`):** Power Drills, Crushers, and Conveyors to mine through the planetary crust.
* **Layer 1 — *Smelt* (`Ingots`):** Resets Ore and Drills to unlock **Alloys** (combining two base stats into custom permanent bonuses) plus drill automation milestones.
* **Layer 2 — *Mantle Breach* (`Magma`):** Introduces **Pressure Risk/Reward**. Let Pressure climb during a run for explosive exponential output—or vent it safely to bank Magma before overheating forces an emergency reset.
* **Layer 3 — *Geode Resonance* (`Prisms`):** Socket colored Prisms into a **3×3 Resonance Grid** where adjacent Prisms multiply each other based on placement and orientation.
* **Layer 4 — *Singularity Core* (`Dark Matter`):** Invert planetary gravity to flatten generator cost scaling and automate multi-layer prestiges.

**What makes it original:** Strong physical progression fantasy where every layer gives the player a distinct new toy (*Alloys* → *Pressure pushing* → *3×3 Prism grid*).
